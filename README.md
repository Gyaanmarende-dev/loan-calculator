# loan-calculator
A small C program that works out the monthly payment on loan
## How it works
You enter the loan amount,the yearly interest rates and the number of months.It shows the monthly payment,the total paid back and a month by month table
## why I built it
During my internship at Equity bank after highschool,I noticed how tiresome it was for clients from traveling long distances and making long queues just for a small service as a loan statement which I believe can be done at the comfort of ones home
